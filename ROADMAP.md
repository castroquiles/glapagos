# GLAPAGOS Roadmap

Milestones for the first 365 days and beyond.

This roadmap is a planning document, not a guarantee. Milestones are
based on reasonable assumptions about contributor availability and
institutional engagement. They are revised quarterly by the Executive
Secretariat in consultation with the Technical Council.

---

## Governing Constraints

Institutional trust takes time. Research institutions, governments, and
multilateral organizations move deliberately. The timeline for formal
membership agreements and ratified governance frameworks is measured in
months to years.

Community contributions are voluntary. Velocity is inherently variable.

Funding precedes scale. Compute-intensive work, full-time staff, and
in-person convenings require funding. Milestones in these areas are
conditional on achieving corresponding funding milestones.

Quality over speed. A governance framework ratified by three institutions
with genuine commitment is more valuable than one ratified by thirty that
have not read it.

## Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-30)

Objective: Establish the technical and institutional foundation.
Make the repository credible to serious external reviewers.

Technical:
[ ] Repository initialized with all core governance documents
[ ] CI/CD pipelines operational
[ ] Contribution infrastructure complete
[ ] Core SDK skeleton published with initial API specification
[ ] Docker Compose enabling local full-stack development
[ ] Security policy in place
[ ] Documentation site deployed from repository
[ ] Initial test suite with minimum 70 percent coverage
[ ] Semantic versioning and release process documented

Governance:
[ ] Constitution published in English and Spanish
[ ] Governance Framework published in English and Spanish
[ ] All supporting governance documents published
[ ] Contributor License Agreement documented
[ ] Conflict of interest disclosure process documented

Community:
[ ] GitHub Discussions enabled and organized by domain
[ ] First five founding institutional members identified
[ ] Working Group charters drafted for WG-LANG, WG-SAFETY, WG-GOV
[ ] First public announcement posted on GLAPAGOS platform

Measurable outcome at Day 30: The repository is in a state that a
senior researcher or policymaker reviewing it for the first time would
assess as serious, credible, and worth engaging with.

---

## Phase 2: Activation (Days 31-90)

Objective: Attract initial community, complete founding governance,
demonstrate technical progress.

Technical:
[ ] SDK v0.1.0 released with documentation
[ ] CLI v0.1.0 released
[ ] Initial API endpoints deployed to staging
[ ] Benchmark suite v0.1.0 for at least one evaluation domain
[ ] First public dataset in the Data Commons with full provenance
[ ] Infrastructure-as-Code for cloud deployment published
[ ] Monitoring stack configured in staging

Governance:
[ ] General Assembly constituted (minimum 5 institutions, 3 countries)
[ ] Technical Council constituted
[ ] EGB nominees announced and open for public comment
[ ] WG-LANG, WG-SAFETY, WG-GOV formally chartered and meeting
[ ] First General Assembly meeting held, minutes published
[ ] Partnership conversations with IDB, UNDP Digital4Dev Hub,
    and two additional multilateral organizations

Research:
[ ] First technical report published
[ ] Call for research proposals issued for two priority areas
[ ] Pre-registration template published

Community:
[ ] Minimum 25 contributors from minimum 5 countries
[ ] Spanish-language community infrastructure operational
[ ] Country chapter templates published
[ ] First three country chapter leads identified

Measurable outcome at Day 90: Functioning governance, active
contributor community across multiple countries, at least one
substantive technical release.

---

## Phase 3: Growth (Days 91-180)

Objective: Scale the community and technical platform, achieve first
significant research outputs.

Technical:
[ ] SDK v0.2.0 with multilingual support (English, Spanish, Portuguese)
[ ] API v1.0 specification finalized and published
[ ] Federated compute pilot with minimum two partner institutions
[ ] First model contributed to the open Model Registry
[ ] Data Commons reaches minimum 10 documented datasets
[ ] First certification track learning materials complete

Governance:
[ ] Ethics and Governance Board fully constituted
[ ] First EGB report published
[ ] Minimum 10 member institutions from minimum 6 countries
[ ] Formal observer status with minimum one intergovernmental organization
[ ] AI Bill of Rights for the Americas draft published for comment
[ ] First conflict of interest audit completed and published

Research:
[ ] Minimum 3 research outputs published
[ ] First peer-reviewed publication acknowledging GLAPAGOS affiliation
[ ] Research collaboration agreement with minimum one university

Funding:
[ ] First grant application submitted to a development bank
[ ] Membership dues collected from founding members
[ ] Funding transparency report published

Community:
[ ] Minimum 75 contributors from minimum 10 countries
[ ] Active country chapters in minimum 5 countries
[ ] Portuguese-language community infrastructure operational

Measurable outcome at Day 180: A credible institution with genuine
multilateral participation, documented research outputs, and a
governance structure that has demonstrated it works in practice.

---

## Phase 4: Institutionalization (Days 181-365)

Objective: Achieve institutional standing and technical depth that
makes GLAPAGOS a durable platform.

Technical:
[ ] SDK v1.0.0: stable API, full documentation, multilingual support
[ ] Production deployment of core platform infrastructure
[ ] Federated compute system with minimum 3 partner institutions
[ ] Open Model Registry contains minimum 5 contributed models
[ ] Data Commons contains minimum 25 documented datasets
[ ] Two certification tracks fully operational
[ ] Annual security audit completed

Governance:
[ ] First annual General Assembly with full governance body participation
[ ] Constitution ratified by minimum 10 member institutions
[ ] Formal partnership agreements with minimum 3 multilateral organizations
[ ] First annual financial audit published
[ ] First constitutional amendment processed through defined procedure

Research:
[ ] Minimum 10 research outputs published
[ ] Benchmark suite v1.0 covering minimum 3 evaluation domains
[ ] At least one output cited by an international organization or
    government document
[ ] Annual research program announced with defined funding

Funding:
[ ] Operating budget funded for Year 2 through diversity of sources
[ ] No single donor represents more than 25 percent of budget
[ ] At least one development bank grant secured or in advanced discussion
[ ] Membership dues constitute at least 20 percent of operating revenue

Community:
[ ] Minimum 200 contributors from minimum 15 countries
[ ] Active country chapters in minimum 10 countries
[ ] First annual contributor survey completed and published
[ ] French-language infrastructure operational
[ ] At least one Indigenous language community engaged in WG-DATA

Measurable outcome at Day 365: GLAPAGOS is an institution, not a
project. It has a ratified constitution, elected bodies, audited
finances, a growing research record, and a contributor community that
functions independently of any founding individual.

---

## Beyond Year 1

Indicative targets set by the General Assembly:
- Minimum 30 member institutions from minimum 15 countries
- Federated compute available to all member institutions
- AI Bill of Rights for the Americas adopted by at least 3 governments
- GLAPAGOS recognized as a formal partner by at least one UN body
- At least one Indigenous language model released under open license
- Self-sustaining funding with no single source above 25 percent
