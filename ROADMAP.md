# GLAPAGOS Roadmap

**Global Laboratory for AI Progress and Governance: Open Systems**
Last updated: June 2026 | Version: 0.1.0

This roadmap reflects the current planned trajectory of GLAPAGOS as an open,
multilateral AI platform for the Western Hemisphere. Dates are targets, not
guarantees. Progress is tracked via GitHub milestones and public issues.

Community input is welcome. Open an issue tagged `domain: governance` or
`type: research` to propose changes to this roadmap.

---

## Guiding Principle

GLAPAGOS is shared infrastructure for the Americas — not a product company,
not a competitive AI entry, and not the property of any single nation,
institution, or individual. Every milestone below serves that principle.

## What Is Not on This Roadmap

- Proprietary AI products or closed commercial services
- Replacing or competing with any existing multilateral institution
- National political agendas or endorsement of any government AI policy
- Closed governance or non-transparent decision-making
