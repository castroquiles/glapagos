# GLAPAGOS Governance Framework

Version 1.0 | Derived from the GLAPAGOS Constitution

---

## 1. Decision-Making Framework

### 1.1 Decision Tiers

| Tier | Scope         | Body                          | Threshold        | Process                        |
|------|---------------|-------------------------------|------------------|--------------------------------|
| 1    | Constitutional| General Assembly              | 75% supermajority| 60-day comment, then vote      |
| 2    | Strategic     | General Assembly              | 60% supermajority| 30-day comment, then vote      |
| 3    | Operational   | Secretariat or Tech Council   | Simple majority  | Documented deliberation        |
| 4    | Routine       | Staff or Working Groups       | Delegated        | Recorded in log                |

All Tier 1 through Tier 3 decisions are recorded in
community/councils/decision-log.md, published within 72 hours,
accompanied by a rationale document.

### 1.2 Appeals
Any member institution may appeal a Tier 2 or Tier 3 decision within
30 days. Appeals are heard by the Ethics and Governance Board. The
Board's decision on appeals is final.

## 2. The General Assembly

### 2.1 Voting Weights

| Membership Category                          | Votes | Cap                              |
|----------------------------------------------|-------|----------------------------------|
| Research University / Academic Institution   | 2     | None                             |
| National Government Observer                 | 1     | 1 per country                    |
| Intergovernmental Organization               | 2     | None                             |
| Civil Society Organization                   | 1     | None                             |
| Private Sector Partner                       | 1     | 20% of total votes collectively  |

Private sector votes are capped collectively at 20 percent of total
votes cast in any General Assembly decision.

### 2.2 Quorum
- Institutions from at least 3 of 4 sub-regions
- Minimum 30 percent of eligible voters
- At least one institution from a lower-income country (World Bank
  classification)

### 2.3 Meeting Procedures
Annual in-person meeting. Host rotates by sub-region on a four-year
cycle. Extraordinary sessions called by the Secretary-General, petition
of 25 percent of members, or unanimous EGB vote.

All sessions are hybrid. Simultaneous English and Spanish. Portuguese
and French interpretation provided where feasible.

## 3. The Technical Council

### 3.1 Composition
- One elected representative per active Working Group
- Three at-large members appointed by the General Assembly
- One seat for representatives of Indigenous communities
- Chief Technical Officer (non-voting, ex officio)

### 3.2 Technical Standards Process
1. Draft published for community comment (minimum 30 days)
2. Technical Council reviews and issues revised draft
3. Second comment period (14 days) if substantive changes made
4. Technical Council vote (simple majority)
5. Standard published and entered into registry

---

## 4. The Ethics and Governance Board

### 4.1 Mandate
- Compliance of all GLAPAGOS activities with Foundational Principles
- Research ethics review for high-risk projects
- Investigation of complaints from contributors, members, or public
- Annual assessment of GLAPAGOS institutional health

### 4.2 Composition
Seven members, staggered four-year terms. No more than two from any
one country. No member holds a formal position in any member institution
during tenure.

### 4.3 Procedures
Meets quarterly. Deliberations are closed; findings are published.
May issue public findings, require formal responses within 60 days,
and refer matters to the General Assembly.

## 5. Working Groups

### Active Working Groups

WG-LANG: Language and Multilingual Systems
Scope: Multilingual model development, Indigenous language inclusion,
dialectal representation, translation infrastructure.

WG-SAFETY: AI Safety and Evaluation
Scope: Red-teaming methodology, evaluation benchmarks, incident
reporting, safety frameworks.

WG-GOV: AI Governance and Policy
Scope: Regulatory frameworks, interoperability standards, AI bills of
rights, public sector adoption.

WG-COMPUTE: Federated Compute Infrastructure
Scope: Shared compute protocols, resource allocation, federated learning.

WG-DATA: Data Commons and Standards
Scope: Open datasets, data governance, quality standards, Indigenous
data sovereignty.

WG-CIVIC: Civic Technology and Public Sector AI
Scope: Government AI applications, civic participation tools, public
interest deployments.

WG-EDU: Education and Workforce Development
Scope: AI literacy curricula, researcher training, certification.

### Working Group Requirements
Each Working Group maintains a public charter, publishes meeting minutes
within one week, produces at minimum one substantive output per quarter,
and reports to the Technical Council monthly.

A Working Group with no substantive output in 12 months is subject to
automatic review.

## 6. Conflict of Interest Policy

All governance participants disclose annually:
- Employment and institutional affiliation
- Funding received from any organization with interests in GLAPAGOS
- Equity ownership above 1 percent in AI-relevant companies
- Advisory roles and board memberships

Participants with a direct financial interest in a decision recuse
from that decision. Recusal is recorded in the decision log.

Private sector members may not chair the General Assembly, Ethics and
Governance Board, or Technical Council.

---

## 7. Language and Accessibility Policy

English and Spanish are official languages. All Tier 1 and Tier 2
decisions are documented in both languages.

Portuguese and French are recognized working languages. All public
documentation is maintained in formats accessible to people with
disabilities. Governance meetings have captioning. Video recordings
have transcripts.

---

## 8. Transparency and Publication Policy

Published by default:
- All meeting minutes (within one week)
- All decisions and rationales (within 72 hours)
- Annual financial accounts (within six months of fiscal year end)
- Ethics and Governance Board findings (immediately upon adoption)
- Working Group outputs (upon completion)

Confidentiality permitted only for: personnel matters,
security-sensitive disclosures, pre-publication research under embargo
no longer than 90 days, and active legal matters.
