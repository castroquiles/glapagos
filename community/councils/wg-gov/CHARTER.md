# WG-GOV Charter
## Working Group on AI Governance and Policy

Version 1.0 | Status: Active

---

## Scope

WG-GOV coordinates work on AI governance frameworks, regulatory
analysis, and policy development with specific relevance to the
Americas. This includes:

- Regulatory interoperability across hemisphere jurisdictions
- AI Bill of Rights for the Americas development and iteration
- Public sector AI adoption frameworks
- International cooperation frameworks
- Analysis of existing national AI strategies in the hemisphere
- Responsible AI principles adapted to regional context

WG-GOV does not develop binding regulations. It produces frameworks,
analyses, and recommendations for adoption by governments and
institutions.

---

## Deliverables (First Six Months)

1. Comparative analysis of national AI strategies across the Americas
2. AI Bill of Rights for the Americas v0.2 incorporating public comment
3. Draft regulatory interoperability framework for two or more
   hemisphere jurisdictions
4. Public sector AI adoption checklist

---

## Membership

Open to all GLAPAGOS Contributors with relevant expertise in law,
policy, governance, or public administration.

Current lead: To be elected at first meeting.
Meeting schedule: Monthly, hybrid format.
Minutes: Published within one week of each meeting.

---

## Relationship to Other Working Groups

WG-GOV coordinates with WG-SAFETY on safety governance frameworks,
with WG-CIVIC on public sector deployment standards, and with
WG-DATA on data governance policy.
