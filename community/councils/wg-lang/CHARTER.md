# WG-LANG Charter
## Working Group on Language and Multilingual Systems

Version 1.0 | Status: Active

---

## Scope

WG-LANG coordinates work on multilingual AI systems with specific
relevance to the Americas. This includes:

- Multilingual model development and evaluation
- Indigenous language inclusion and preservation
- Dialectal variation across Spanish, Portuguese, French, and
  Haitian Creole as spoken in the hemisphere
- Translation infrastructure for platform and governance documents
- Low-resource language NLP methodology

WG-LANG does not cover general NLP research without regional relevance.

---

## Deliverables (First Six Months)

1. Inventory of Indigenous languages of the Americas with existing
   NLP resources, data, and active research groups
2. Survey of dialectal variation relevant to hemisphere AI deployment
3. Draft data contribution guidelines for low-resource languages
4. Evaluation framework for multilingual systems in Americas context

---

## Membership

Open to all GLAPAGOS Contributors and above with relevant expertise.
Minimum five members required to maintain active status.

Current lead: To be elected at first meeting.
Meeting schedule: Monthly, hybrid format.
Minutes: Published within one week of each meeting.

---

## Relationship to Other Working Groups

WG-LANG coordinates with WG-DATA on dataset standards for language
corpora, with WG-SAFETY on evaluation of multilingual safety
properties, and with WG-EDU on multilingual education materials.
