# WG-SAFETY Charter
## Working Group on AI Safety and Evaluation

Version 1.0 | Status: Active

---

## Scope

WG-SAFETY coordinates work on AI safety evaluation methodology,
red-teaming practice, incident reporting, and safety frameworks
with specific relevance to the Americas. This includes:

- Safety evaluation benchmarks for hemisphere-specific risks
- Red-teaming methodology and documentation standards
- Incident reporting infrastructure and taxonomy
- Safety review processes for models in the GLAPAGOS registry
- Analysis of safety failures with regional context

---

## Deliverables (First Six Months)

1. Safety evaluation checklist for models entering the GLAPAGOS
   Model Registry
2. Incident reporting taxonomy and submission process
3. AMER-SAFE benchmark v0.1 covering at least three safety domains
4. Red-teaming methodology guide for hemisphere-relevant risks

---

## Membership

Open to all GLAPAGOS Contributors with relevant expertise in AI
safety, security, or risk assessment.

Current lead: To be elected at first meeting.
Meeting schedule: Monthly, hybrid format.
Minutes: Published within one week of each meeting.
