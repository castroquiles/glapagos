# GLAPAGOS Charter

Operational Charter | Derived from the Constitution and Governance Framework

---

## Purpose

This Charter governs the day-to-day operations of GLAPAGOS. It is
subordinate to the Constitution and may be amended by a 60 percent
vote of the General Assembly after a 30-day comment period.

---

## 1. Executive Secretariat Operations

### 1.1 Staff Structure
The Executive Secretariat consists of:
- Secretary-General (elected by General Assembly, four-year term)
- Deputy Secretary-General for Research
- Deputy Secretary-General for Governance and Policy
- Deputy Secretary-General for Technology
- Deputy Secretary-General for Community and Education
- Administrative staff as budget permits

### 1.2 Responsibilities
The Executive Secretariat:
- Manages day-to-day platform operations
- Maintains the public repository and documentation
- Coordinates Working Group scheduling and support
- Manages membership applications and dues collection
- Produces the annual report and financial statements
- Represents GLAPAGOS in external relationships
- Maintains the decision log and governance records

### 1.3 Reporting
The Secretary-General reports to the General Assembly annually and
to the Ethics and Governance Board on request. Monthly operational
summaries are published publicly.

---

## 2. Membership Operations

### 2.1 Application Process
1. Institution submits application via glapagos.com
2. Executive Secretariat reviews for eligibility and alignment
3. Applicant agrees to Membership Agreement, Code of Conduct,
   and Governance Framework
4. Executive Secretariat issues decision within 60 days
5. Declined applications may be appealed to the General Assembly

### 2.2 Dues and Fees
The dues schedule is published at glapagos.com and reviewed annually
by the General Assembly. Dues are structured by institution type and
country income classification (World Bank methodology).

Full fee waivers are available for documented financial need.
Applications are submitted to the Executive Secretariat.

Compute contributions may offset monetary dues up to 50 percent of
the applicable fee at fair market rates.

### 2.3 Member Obligations
Member institutions in good standing:
- Pay dues within 90 days of invoice
- Designate a primary contact and governance delegate
- Submit an annual activity report
- Comply with all GLAPAGOS policies
- Participate in at least one Working Group

## 3. Research Operations

### 3.1 Research Submission
Research outputs produced with GLAPAGOS resources or coordination
are submitted through the platform at glapagos.ai. Submissions include:
- Complete manuscript or technical report
- Data and code sufficient for replication
- Conflict of interest disclosures for all authors
- Ethics review status where applicable

### 3.2 Review Process
Submitted research is reviewed by the Technical Council for
consistency with GLAPAGOS standards. Review is not peer review
of scientific quality; that occurs through standard academic channels.

### 3.3 Publication
All accepted research is published open-access. Preprints are
strongly encouraged. Embargo maximum: 90 days from acceptance.

---

## 4. Working Group Operations

### 4.1 Chartering
Working Groups are chartered by the Technical Council. A charter
proposal requires:
- Clear scope statement
- Initial membership of at least five contributors
- Defined deliverables for the first six months
- Named Working Group lead

### 4.2 Leadership
Working Group leads are elected by Working Group members annually.
No individual may serve as lead of more than two Working Groups
simultaneously. Leads report to the Technical Council.

### 4.3 Meetings
Working Group meetings are open to all Contributors and above.
Meetings are held in a hybrid format. Minutes are published within
one week. Recordings are published where participants consent.

---

## 5. Repository Operations

### 5.1 Branching Strategy
- main: protected, requires PR and review, no direct push
- release/vX.Y: release branches, protected
- feat/*, fix/*, docs/*, chore/*: contributor branches

### 5.2 Review Requirements
- All PRs require at least one approval from a CODEOWNERS designee
- Governance document PRs require two approvals
- Security-sensitive changes require Security team review
- All automated CI checks must pass before merge

### 5.3 Release Cadence
Minor releases: monthly if substantive changes are ready
Patch releases: as needed for bug fixes and security updates
Major releases: as warranted, with migration guide required

---

## 6. Communication Standards

### 6.1 Official Channels
- Primary platform: glapagos.com
- Research platform: glapagos.ai
- Repository: github.com/glapagos-ai/glapagos
- Discussions: GitHub Discussions in the repository

### 6.2 External Communications
Public statements on behalf of GLAPAGOS are made by the
Secretary-General or designated Deputy. Working Group leads may make
statements on matters within their Working Group scope.

### 6.3 Media and Press
Media inquiries are directed to the Executive Secretariat via
glapagos.com. No individual contributor speaks on behalf of GLAPAGOS
without explicit authorization.
