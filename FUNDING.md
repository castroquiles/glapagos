# Funding Policy

---

## Principles

Transparency: All funding sources and amounts are published annually,
within six months of each fiscal year end. No anonymous donations above
$1,000 USD equivalent are accepted.

Diversity: No single source provides more than 25 percent of annual
operating revenue. This is a hard limit, not a target.

Independence: No funding comes with conditions on research publication,
governance decisions, or public statements. Funders may specify the
general domain of a grant but not its findings or conclusions.

Alignment: Funders must be compatible with the GLAPAGOS mission.

Sustainability: Funding strategy aims for a diversified base that does
not depend on the continued participation of any single source.

## Revenue Sources

### Membership Dues

Dues are structured by institution type and country income classification
using the World Bank methodology, reviewed annually.

| Institution Type                              | High Income | Upper-Middle | Lower-Middle | Low Income |
|-----------------------------------------------|-------------|--------------|--------------|------------|
| Research University (large, >10,000 students) | $12,000/yr  | $6,000/yr    | $2,400/yr    | $600/yr    |
| Research University (medium, 3,000-10,000)    | $6,000/yr   | $3,000/yr    | $1,200/yr    | $300/yr    |
| Research Institute or Think Tank              | $8,000/yr   | $4,000/yr    | $1,600/yr    | $400/yr    |
| Government Body (national/federal)            | $15,000/yr  | $7,500/yr    | $3,000/yr    | $750/yr    |
| Civil Society Organization                    | $2,000/yr   | $1,000/yr    | $400/yr      | $100/yr    |
| Private Sector (>1,000 employees)             | $20,000/yr  | $10,000/yr   | $4,000/yr    | $1,000/yr  |
| Private Sector (50-1,000 employees)           | $8,000/yr   | $4,000/yr    | $1,600/yr    | $400/yr    |
| Private Sector (<50 employees)                | $2,000/yr   | $1,000/yr    | $400/yr      | $100/yr    |

Full fee waivers are available for documented financial need.
Compute contributions may offset dues up to 50 percent at fair market rates.

### Grants and Contracts

GLAPAGOS actively seeks grants from:
- Development banks: IDB, CAF, World Bank
- National science foundations and research funding agencies
- Private foundations with relevant missions
- Intergovernmental organizations: UNDP, UNESCO, ITU, OAS

Research contracts are accepted from public sector and international
bodies for defined deliverables, subject to publication and independence
requirements above.

## Financial Governance

Budget Process: Prepared by the Executive Secretariat, reviewed by the
Technical Council, approved by the General Assembly. Published publicly.

Financial Controls:
- Dual-signature for disbursements above defined threshold
- Annual independent financial audit, no conflicts of interest
- Quarterly financial reporting to the General Assembly
- Expense policies consistent with public-benefit organization standards

Reserve Policy: Operating reserve of not less than three months of
operating expenses, held in low-risk instruments.

---

## Donor Recognition

Funders are recognized in the annual report and on the GLAPAGOS website.
Recognition does not imply endorsement of funder products or positions.
Recognition does not confer governance rights beyond those available
through standard membership.

---

## Conflict of Interest in Funding

Any funder that is also a member institution complies with the conflict
of interest policy. Any funding relationship that could appear to create
a conflict in a research or governance decision is disclosed, and
potentially conflicted parties recuse from the relevant decision.
