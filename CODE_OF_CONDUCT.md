# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

GLAPAGOS is a multilateral institution that depends on the voluntary
participation of researchers, developers, policymakers, and community
members across the Western Hemisphere. The quality of that participation
depends on an environment where all contributors can engage productively.

The following standards apply to all GLAPAGOS spaces: GitHub repositories,
discussions, mailing lists, Working Group meetings, and any other forum
operating under GLAPAGOS auspices.

### Expected Behavior

- Engage with disagreements through reasoned argument and evidence
- Treat all participants with professional respect regardless of country
  of origin, language, institution, gender, age, disability status,
  race, ethnicity, religion, or level of experience
- Acknowledge and correct mistakes
- Disclose conflicts of interest
- Prioritize the quality of the work and the health of the institution
  over individual or institutional ego

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- Personal attacks or sustained disparagement of individuals
- Deliberate misrepresentation of others' positions
- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Conduct that would be unacceptable in a professional scientific setting

---

## Enforcement

Reports of unacceptable behavior are sent to conduct@glapagos.com.

All reports are reviewed by the Executive Secretariat. Substantiated
violations result in consequences proportional to severity, ranging
from a formal warning to permanent exclusion from GLAPAGOS participation.

The Ethics and Governance Board has authority to review enforcement
decisions on appeal.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all GLAPAGOS spaces and to conduct
that occurs outside GLAPAGOS spaces where it materially affects the
safety or integrity of GLAPAGOS participation.

---

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version
2.1, with modifications appropriate to GLAPAGOS as a multilateral
research institution.
